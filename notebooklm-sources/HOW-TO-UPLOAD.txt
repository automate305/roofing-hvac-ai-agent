HOW TO UPLOAD THESE FILES TO NOTEBOOKLM
========================================
URL: https://notebooklm.google.com
Sign in with your Google account.


STEP 1 — CREATE A NEW NOTEBOOK
--------------------------------
1. Click "+ New Notebook"
2. Name it: "Roofing & HVAC AI Agent"
3. Click Create


STEP 2 — ADD ALL 5 SOURCE FILES
---------------------------------
For each .txt file below, click "+ Add Source" then choose "Upload file":

1. 01-product-overview.txt
   - What the product is, who it's for, the five problems it solves, business model options

2. 02-live-demo-results.txt
   - Actual test results from June 27, 2026 — every lead, score, response time, and analytics output

3. 03-capabilities-by-use-case.txt
   - What the agent does for roofing companies and HVAC companies, scenario by scenario

4. 04-faq.txt
   - 18 Q&As covering every question a contractor prospect is likely to ask

5. 05-roadmap-and-integrations.txt
   - Phase 1/2/3 feature breakdown, Twilio/SendGrid/OpenAI/Stripe requirements, Make.com scenarios

Upload all 5. NotebookLM synthesizes across all sources when answering questions.


STEP 3 — SUGGESTED FIRST PROMPTS TO TRY
-----------------------------------------
After uploading, try these in the chat panel:

"Create a sales one-pager I can send to a roofing contractor."

"What are the most important things to tell an HVAC company owner about this system?"

"Write a 60-second elevator pitch for this product."

"What does Phase 2 add and how long will it take?"

"Generate a FAQ for a skeptical contractor who's never used software before."

"What integrations are needed and what do they cost?"

"Compare what this does for roofing companies vs HVAC companies."

"Create a checklist for onboarding a new contractor client."

"Write an email I can send to a roofing company introducing this product."

"What are the known gaps and when will they be fixed?"


STEP 4 — USE THE NOTEBOOK GUIDE FEATURE
-----------------------------------------
Click "Notebook Guide" in the right panel. NotebookLM will auto-generate:
- A summary of all sources
- Key topics
- An FAQ based on the content
- A study guide / briefing document

These are useful as pitch decks, client handouts, or internal training documents.


STEP 5 — GENERATE AN AUDIO OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------
Click "Audio Overview" in the Notebook Guide panel. NotebookLM will create a 10-15 minute podcast-style conversation between two AI hosts explaining the product. Useful for:
- Playing for a contractor prospect during a call
- Training your sales team
- Personal review while commuting
